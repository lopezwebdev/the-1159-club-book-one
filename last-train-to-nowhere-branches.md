# The 11:59 Club — Book 1: The Last Train to Nowhere
*A Choose-Your-Scare Adventure*

### Branch Scenes Draft (B1–B8)

These are shorter than the golden-path scenes on purpose — connective tissue that either loops back into the spine or peels off toward an ending. Same placeholder names: **Rin**, **Kenji**, **Mara**.

---

### B1 — One More Stop
*From G2, staying seated instead of jumping off.*

Fine. No big deal. You'll just get off at the next stop instead.

The train rattles on. One minute. Two. The lights inside flicker once, and outside the window there's nothing — no platforms sliding past, no tunnel walls, just dark.

The speaker crackles again. Same garbled voice. *"Next stop —"*

The doors open onto the exact same platform. Same flickering light. Same hum from a vending machine down the way.

You didn't gain a second chance. You just took the long way to the same place.

**> Get off here, for real this time.** *(→ G3)*
**> Stay seated again and see what happens.** *(→ E7, if repeated)*

---

### B2 — The Stairs That Loop
*From G3, heading for the exit stairs without checking the vending machine.*

You take the stairs two at a time, glad to be moving, glad to have a plan.

The stairs turn. Then turn again. Then a third time, and you're fairly sure staircases aren't supposed to have this many turns for one flight.

You come out — onto the same platform you just left. Same flicker. Same hum.

Okay. Different plan needed.

**> Actually check out that vending machine this time.** *(→ G4)*
**> Try the stairs again — maybe you missed a turn.** *(short loop, 1 extra beat, then funnels back to G4 regardless)*

---

### B3 — Pressing Rin
*From G5, pushing Rin for what she meant instead of asking how to get out.*

"What do you mean I already got off this train once? I've never even *been* here—"

**> Push harder. She knows something and you want it now.** *(→ E6)*
**> Back off. Whatever this is, it's clearly not something she wants to explain yet.** *(→ G6, but Rin stays quieter for the rest of the story)*

---

### B4 — The Shifting Map
*From G6, checking the station map instead of trusting Rin's directions.*

The map is bolted to the wall, laminated, official-looking. **YOU ARE HERE**, it says, with a little red dot.

You look at the exits. Blink. Look again.

The exits have moved.

**> Look one more time, just to be sure you're not imagining it.** *(→ E4, if repeated)*
**> Stop looking at it and go find Rin instead.** *(→ G6, loses a beat but rejoins the spine)*

---

### B5 — Opening the Booth Early
*From G7, trying the conductor's booth door instead of walking past.*

You know you probably shouldn't. You try it anyway.

The lock gives with no resistance at all — like it was never really locked, just waiting for you to ask.

**> Step inside.** *(→ E3)*
**> Change your mind at the last second and shut the door again.** *(→ G7, rejoins spine — but the booth remembers you tried, and reappears "ajar on its own" faster at G11)*

---

### B6 — Leaving Mara
*From G8, telling Mara to wait instead of helping her.*

"I'll get help," you say. "Just — stay here. I'll be back."

She doesn't look like she believes you. Honestly, you're not sure you believe you either.

You keep walking. The panic in her voice fades behind you, and something about how easy that was bothers you more than the station does.

**> Keep going. You made a choice, now live with it.** *(→ G10, story continues cold-toned, sets up E2/E8)*
**> Turn back — you can't actually leave her there.** *(→ G9, rejoins spine, help arrives late but arrives)*

---

### B7 — Walking Past the Booth
*From G11, walking past the ajar booth door instead of looking inside.*

Whatever's behind that door, you don't want to know tonight. You keep walking.

Behind you, faint, you could swear you hear the door creak shut on its own — like it's disappointed.

**> Keep moving toward the platform.** *(→ G13, skips G12's clue — the "tell" for spotting the real train is harder to catch without it)*
**> Actually, go back and look. You'll regret not knowing.** *(→ G12, rejoins spine fully)*

---

### B8 — Checking the Platform Yourself
*From G13, watching for the tell instead of trusting Rin outright.*

*⏱ 0:10 — forgiving: timing out defaults to trusting Rin.*

Two lights down the tunnel. Both trains, growing louder, closer, identical in every way you can see.

*(If you have the item from G4/G10: its second clue kicks in here — something about one set of headlights doesn't match. You catch it fast.)*

*(If you don't have the item: you're watching, waiting, running out of ways to tell them apart — and running out of time.)*

**> You spot it — the real train is the one with [the tell].** *(→ G14, clean)*
**> You can't tell. Trust Rin's earlier instructions instead.** *(→ G14, same outcome, but Rin's warning at G13 does the work instead of the reader's own observation)*

## Notes for revision pass
- Every branch scene is short — 1 to 3 short paragraphs — by design. The reader should feel like they took a detour, not like they started a new chapter.
- Several branches (B2, B4, B7) are built to be *repeatable* without punishing the reader on the first pass — they only escalate to a real ending (E4, E7, etc.) if the reader repeats the same wrong choice a second time. That reward-for-curiosity, cost-for-stubbornness pattern is what should make replaying fun rather than annoying.
- B6 is the emotional hinge of the whole map — it's the only branch that quietly recolors the rest of the story (cold tone into G10 onward) without announcing itself. Worth giving it a subtle visual or audio cue in the build (a color shift, a muted sound) so kids feel the tonal change even before they consciously register why.
- B3 and B5 are the two "point of no return within a single playthrough" branches — once taken, they lock into their ending rather than allowing a rejoin. Keep those decisive; they're what makes E6 and E3 feel earned rather than arbitrary.
