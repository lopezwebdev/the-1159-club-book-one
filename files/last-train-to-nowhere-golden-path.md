# The 11:59 Club — Book 1: The Last Train to Nowhere
*A Choose-Your-Scare Adventure*

### Golden Path Draft (G1–G15)
Placeholder names used: **YOU** (protagonist, 2nd person), **RIN** (the girl), **KENJI** (the friend, via text), **MARA** (the other trapped kid). Swap freely — flagged in brackets where a real name would go.

---

### G1 — The Last Car

The train is almost empty. Just you, a sleeping salaryman two seats down, and the hum of wheels on track.

Your phone buzzes.

**KENJI:** *did you seriously just leave? i needed to talk to you*

You stare at it a second too long. You told him you'd stay after practice. You didn't.

**> Text him back and explain.**
**> Ghost him. You'll deal with it tomorrow.**

*[10-second flavor timer here on replay/hard mode only — first playthrough, no pressure.]*

---

### G2 — Doors Closing

*⏱ 0:10*

*"Next stop —"* the speaker crackles, garbled. You don't catch the name.

The doors chime. You weren't even paying attention to which stop this was supposed to be.

**> Jump off now, before the doors seal shut.**
**> Stay seated — you'll catch the next one back.**

The doors hiss open. Ten seconds. Nine. Eight —

You grab your bag and jump.

The doors slam shut behind you like they'd been waiting for you to move.

---

### G3 — The Empty Platform

The train pulls away, and the platform goes quiet in a way platforms aren't supposed to be quiet.

No one else got off. Not the salaryman. Not the two girls who were laughing by the doors a minute ago. Just you.

The overhead light nearest you flickers, buzzing like it's thinking about giving up entirely. Down the platform, a **vending machine** glows — the only steady light in the whole station.

**> Go look at the vending machine.**
**> Head straight for the stairs and get out of here.**

---

### G4 — The Machine That Knows You

The machine is stocked with drinks and snacks you don't recognize — brands with no logos, wrappers a little too shiny. One item near the bottom row glows faintly, like it's lit from the inside.

There's no coin slot. No card reader. Just a button.

**> Press it. Take the item.**
**> Leave it. Something about this feels like a trade you don't want to make.**

*(If taken: a small object drops — a keychain flashlight, a foil-wrapped candy, whatever fits your visual style. It's warm in your hand, like it's been held before.)*

You slide it into your pocket and keep moving.

---

### G5 — The Girl on the Bench

A girl about your age is sitting alone on a bench near the stairs, like she's been waiting for exactly you.

She doesn't look scared. She looks *tired.*

**RIN:** "You already got off this train once."

You don't know what that means. You don't know her. But she's the only other person in this entire station.

**> Ask her what she means.**
**> Ask her how to get out of here.**

---

### G6 — Two Halls

Rin doesn't really answer either question — not directly.

**RIN:** "There's a way out. It's not the stairs. It's not the way you think." She points down two hallways — one dim but lit, one completely dark. "Go that way. Don't look at the map on the wall. Don't trust it."

**> Follow her directions down the lit hallway.**
**> Check the station map first, just to be sure.**

You follow her. Whatever the map does, you don't want to find out tonight.

---

### G7 — The Locked Booth

Halfway down the hall sits a stopped train car, dark inside except for one dim light. Painted on the side: CONDUCTOR — DO NOT ENTER.

The door is locked. From the *outside.*

Something inside shifts. Slowly. Like it heard you.

**> Try the door anyway.**
**> Keep walking. Whatever's in there can wait.**

Every instinct says walk. So you walk.

---

### G8 — The Gate

Further down, a turnstile gate blocks the hall — and behind it, a kid about your age is gripping the bars, panicked.

**MARA:** "Please — it won't open, I've been stuck since the last stop, please—"

**> Help her get free.**
**> Tell her to wait here — you'll go find help.**

You didn't come all this way to leave someone behind. You start working the gate.

---

### G9 — The Alarm

*⏱ 0:15*

The gate groans and something in the wall *clicks* — a chime starts, low and rising, lights along the hallway beginning to strobe.

**> Pull her through the gap now.**
**> Grab the loose bolt and widen the gap first — slower, but safer.**

However you do it, Mara scrambles free, breathing hard. "Thank you — thank you, I thought — "

"Later," you say. "Let's move."

---

### G10 — Your Name in Lights

You pass the vending machine again on the way back — except now the little screen above the buttons is flickering with letters.

It's spelling your name.

*(If you took the item earlier: a second word flickers beneath it — a hint, a direction, a warning. If you didn't: the screen just flickers your name, over and over, and says nothing else.)*

Mara tugs your sleeve. "Don't stare at it too long."

**> Take her advice and move on.**
**> One more second — just to see what it does.**

You look away. Whatever it wanted, it doesn't get it tonight.

---

### G11 — The Booth, Ajar

Back near the conductor's booth, the door is different now. Not locked. Just barely open, a sliver of dark inside, like something wants you to look.

**> Look inside.**
**> Walk past it toward the platform. You're almost out.**

You step closer and look.

---

### G12 — What's Inside

Inside the booth: an old ticket stub, a photo gone soft at the corners, a note in handwriting that looks — unsettlingly — a little like Rin's.

It isn't a trap. It's a *warning.* Left by someone who didn't make it out in time.

Rin appears behind you, quieter than before. "I've been here longer than you think," she says. "I'm not doing this *to* you."

**> Ask her what happens now.**
**> Just nod. There isn't time for more questions.**

---

### G13 — Real Train, Fake Train

*⏱ 0:10 (forgiving — timing out defaults to trusting Rin)*

"A train's coming," Rin says. "But so is a fake one. They'll look the same. Almost."

**> Trust her completely — board when she tells you to.**
**> Watch the platform yourself, just to be sure you can spot the difference.**

*(If you have the item, its second clue from G10 confirms what to look for — a flicker in the headlights, a missing sound, whatever fits your visual language. Without it, you're trusting instinct and Rin.)*

Down the tunnel, light approaches. Two lights, actually. One real. One not.

---

### G14 — Mara's Choice

Mara's beside you now, watching the tunnel too.

**MARA:** "Are you — are we going together?"

**> Bring her onto the train with you.**
**> Let her find her own way out.**

You reach out and grab her sleeve. Whatever this place is, nobody else gets left on this platform tonight.

---

### G15 — The Doors Open

The right train slows to a stop. The doors open with an ordinary, boring, *wonderful* hiss.

Rin doesn't move to follow.

**> Step on board.**
**> Hesitate one more second and look back at the station.**

You step on. The doors close behind you and Mara.

Rin stays on the platform, small and still, watching the train pull away — like she's watched it pull away before.
